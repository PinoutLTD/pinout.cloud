IKEA Smart Home and Home Assistant

The package combines IKEA Smart Home devices with the power of Home Assistant, creating a single platform for managing lighting, sensors, and automations. Thanks to Zigbee connectivity and open standards, the system can be expanded with additional devices and integrations as your needs grow.

A local Home Assistant server provides fast device response, reliable operation, and a high level of data privacy. All automations run within your home network without constant dependence on external cloud services.

The system supports custom automation scenarios for everyday tasks, scheduled lighting control, sensor-based notifications, and centralized management of all connected devices through a single interface.

The open ecosystem makes it easy to integrate additional lighting, climate control, security, and home monitoring equipment in the future without replacing the existing system.
